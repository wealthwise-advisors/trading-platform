1. What this service is

AutoTrader is a private platform for backtesting and analysing futures and equities strategies against historical market data. It charts price, runs strategies over past bars, and reports what those rules would have done.

2. Backtests are hypothetical

A backtest applies rules to data that has already happened, with perfect knowledge of the bars and no real money at risk. Results are hypothetical and not a prediction.

  • Past performance does not indicate future results.
