Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

The short version. Only what an account needs is collected. Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking anywhere in this application.

1. What is collected

DataWhy
UsernameIdentifies your account and your data.
Full nameShown when you are signed in.
Email addressConfirming the account, resetting a password, recovering a username.
Country · phone · date of birthOptional profile details. Nothing is required to use the platform.
IP addressRate limiting. Without it, one machine could exhaust sign-in or reset attempts for everybody.
Browser user-agentRecorded with a session so you can recognise your own sign-ins.
Your backtests and settingsThe work you create here. Only ever visible to you.
Saved configurationsThe named sidebar setups you save, stored against your account so they follow you between devices. They are deleted with the account.

2. How it is stored

  • Passwords are hashed with argon2id and never stored in a readable form. Nobody, including the operator, can read your password.
  • Sessions, password-reset links, email confirmations and pending sign-in handles are stored as a SHA-256 of the value, never the value itself — a copy of the database yields no working session and no usable reset link.
  • Traffic is served over HTTPS. Session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, so they are unreadable by scripts and are not sent from other sites.

3. Your data is yours alone

Backtests, settings and results are scoped to the account that created them. Requesting another account's work returns not found, not forbidden — a "forbidden" would confirm the item exists.

4. Third parties

There are exactly three kinds, and each receives only what its job requires:

WhoReceives
Google · LinkedIn · GitHub · X Only if you choose that button. They tell us a permanent account id, your name, and — except X, which shares no address at any scope — a verified email. We send them nothing about you.
Resend Your address and the message, to deliver confirmation, password reset and username reminder mail.
Cloudflare Turnstile A bot check on the registration form. It sees the challenge, not your form.

There is no analytics provider, no advertising network and no tracking pixel in this application. Nothing here follows you between sites, and nothing you do is measured for marketing.

Crash reporting is built in but switched off. The application can send a report when a page fails to render, so that a fault can be found and fixed rather than waiting for somebody to describe it. It is dormant unless a reporting endpoint is configured, and when it is, a report carries only: the error message and its stack, the page path you were on, your browser's user-agent string, the release, and the time. It never carries your username, your email address, your password, your session cookie, the contents of any request, or the query part of a URL — that last one deliberately, because reset and confirmation links carry their token there.

5. How long it is kept

  • Account details — until the account is closed, which you can do yourself at any time.
  • Sessions — until they expire, or immediately when you sign out. Changing your password destroys every session at once.
  • Reset and confirmation links — single use, and short-lived whether used or not.
  • Pending sign-in handles — twenty minutes.

6. What you can ask for

You can close your account yourself, at any time, from Account → Close account once you are signed in. It removes the account, every backtest and trade recorded under it, and signs you out everywhere. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

You can download a copy of your data yourself, from Account → Download my data. It is a JSON file containing your account details, your saved configurations, your backtests and their trades, and the record of where your account has been signed in. Passwords and session keys are never included — they are credentials, not information about you.

Write to snandepu9@gmail.com if you would rather we did either of those for you, or to have anything corrected.

7. Children

This service is not intended for anyone under 18, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.
